# Improvement Roadmap

## Guiding Priorities

- Build credibility before social engagement.
- Prioritize verified performance data over popularity signals.
- Keep the MVP narrow enough to pilot with real schools, clubs, or institutions.
- Protect athlete privacy and safety from day one.
- Capture structured data early so AI and analytics can be added later.

## Phase 1: Core MVP

Deliver a usable platform for athlete evidence and recruiter discovery.

- User registration and role-based onboarding
- Athlete profile management
- Evidence upload with metadata
- Coach and institution verification workflow
- Initial LevelPlay Rank
- Basic search and discovery
- Notifications for verification and recruiter activity
- Admin moderation and role management
- Pilot with a small number of schools, clubs, or institutions

## Phase 2: Enhanced Discovery

Improve search quality and recruiter workflows.

- Advanced filtering by sport, location, age, gender, position, verification status, and rank tier
- Scout and agent shortlists
- Saved searches and alerts
- Geographic discovery insights
- Better profile summaries and credibility indicators
- Structured metadata improvements for future AI analysis

## Phase 3: Social and Engagement

Add controlled engagement without weakening ranking fairness.

- Athlete storytelling and profile highlights
- Comments or limited interactions with moderation
- Fan following and community features
- Progress badges and ranking tiers
- Community challenges with safeguards against popularity bias

## Phase 4: AI and Analytics

Introduce AI only after enough clean verified data exists.

- Video analysis for sport-specific metrics
- Key moment tagging and labelled training data
- Recommendation models for scouts and agents
- Performance dashboards for athletes, coaches, institutions, and recruiters
- Fairness monitoring across regions, age groups, gender, and sport categories

## Phase 5: Expansion and Monetization

Scale the platform commercially and operationally.

- Premium recruiter tools
- Sponsorship and scholarship programs
- Payment gateways for premium services
- Partnerships with sporting organizations, academies, broadcasters, and schools
- Wearable integrations for GPS, heart rate, and advanced metrics
- Live streaming and highlight distribution

## Near-Term Improvements

- Define final role permissions before implementation.
- Publish clear LevelPlay Rank guidance for users.
- Add visible verification states on athlete profiles.
- Ensure athlete privacy controls are granular and understandable.
- Add audit trails for verification, endorsements, moderation, and ranking updates.
- Use responsive UI patterns from the prototype but simplify onboarding for first-time users.
