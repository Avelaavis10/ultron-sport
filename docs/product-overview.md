# Product Overview

## Mission

Ultron Sport exists to make grassroots and semi-professional sporting talent visible, verified, and discoverable.

Many athletes lack exposure because scouting networks are uneven, costly, and often based on personal access rather than verified performance. Ultron Sport gives athletes a structured way to present credible evidence and gives recruiters a trusted way to discover talent.

## Problem

- Athletes struggle to prove ability beyond word of mouth, social media clips, or local reputation.
- Scouts and agents need faster ways to discover credible prospects.
- Coaches and institutions need a reliable workflow for validating athlete performance.
- Popularity-based platforms can reward attention instead of verified sporting evidence.
- Rural and under-resourced athletes may have limited connectivity, visibility, and access to opportunities.

## Solution

Ultron Sport provides a mobile-first platform where athletes create profiles, upload evidence, request verification, and build a LevelPlay Rank. Coaches, schools, clubs, and institutions validate evidence. Scouts and agents use search, filters, rankings, and shortlists to discover suitable athletes.

## MVP Value Proposition

- Athletes get a credible digital sporting profile.
- Coaches and institutions help strengthen trust in the athlete record.
- Scouts and agents reduce search cost and improve talent discovery.
- Administrators maintain fairness, safety, and compliance.
- The platform captures structured evidence needed for future AI analysis.

## User Roles

- Athlete: uploads evidence, manages profile, requests verification, views ranking, receives opportunities.
- Coach: validates evidence, manages team or school rosters, endorses performance.
- Scout or Agent: searches athletes, saves shortlists, records evaluations, initiates contact.
- Institution: registers teams, assigns coaches, manages athlete groups, contributes event information.
- Fan: follows athletes and engages with community features without directly controlling rank.
- Administrator: manages roles, moderation, compliance, audits, and system health.

## MVP Success Criteria

- Athletes can register, create profiles, and upload evidence.
- Evidence can move through a clear verification lifecycle.
- Verified evidence contributes to LevelPlay Rank.
- Scouts can search and filter athletes by useful criteria.
- Athletes understand why their rank changed.
- The system protects personal data and enforces role-based access.
- The MVP can be piloted with a small number of schools, clubs, or institutions.

## Future Opportunities

- AI-assisted video analysis and performance recommendations
- Live streaming and highlight reels
- Wearable integrations for GPS, heart rate, and advanced metrics
- Community challenges and competitions
- Scholarship, academy, sponsor, and broadcaster partnerships
- Premium recruiter tools and monetization models
